Current simulation assumptions

Choose a scenario to see its documented model limits.

Response vehicles use Kenney CC0 models as visual representations. Unit counts are modelled, operational availability is unknown, and the vehicle display scale grows at overview distance.

Data & readiness CHECKING

    Sentinel-1 outlines are radar classifications tuned to the mapped OSM river in the before image. They are observed-image evidence with classification uncertainty, not independently validated ground truth. Relative-stage scenarios are hypothetical, not forecasts.

    Google 2023 building heights are model estimates; valid footprint medians take priority, with labelled OSM fallback. Missing heights remain empty. OSM and Google source dates differ.

    Matte building roofs and procedural facades are display representations. Heights, masks and metrics come only from the named scientific and mapped sources.

    WorldPop 2020 models residential population, not live occupancy or observed evacuations. Its approximately 100 m cells are allocated to the simulation grid without adding demographic detail. Incomplete coverage is kept explicit.
